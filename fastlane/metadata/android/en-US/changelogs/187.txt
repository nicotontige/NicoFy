First release of NicoFy, a fork of Musify 10.6.0 that now goes its own way.

- New name and app id (com.nicotontige.nicofy): it installs next to Musify, not over it. Export your data from Musify (Settings > Backup) and import it here.
- Updates now come from github.com/nicotontige/NicoFy
- Queue kept across app restarts
- Chapter navigation inside long compilations
- Search results sorted behind category tabs
- Playback buffered to a file, so a slow network is not heard
- "Made for kids" videos play
- Back restarts the song when pressed mid-song
- Next works when the queue is empty
- Android Auto: like button and played-songs marker
- Android TV support
- Library: sorting, batch selection, offline status on each song
- Scrobbling to Last.fm and ListenBrainz
