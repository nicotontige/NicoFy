New
- Local sync: keep your library in step between your NicoFy devices over Wi-Fi, with no account and no cloud. Pair two devices with a six-digit PIN, then choose what to exchange: liked songs, playlists and folders, recently played, listening recap, search history, liked radio stations. Settings > Local sync.
- Android Auto: every playlist, and the Liked, Downloaded and Recently played lists, now open with a "Shuffle" row that plays the whole list in random order, like the shuffle button on the phone.

Fixes
- Android Auto: when the car reopened the app, the saved queue was sometimes lost: only the last song came back and the automatic song picker filled in the rest. The whole queue now comes back, where you left it.
- Listening stats no longer count time spent buffering, and the song playing when the month's history is full is no longer dropped.
- Old cache entries are cleaned up at start-up.
- Plain HTTP is now allowed only for the player's own local proxy, not for the whole app.
- Built with Flutter 3.50.
